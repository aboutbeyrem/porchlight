Porchlight

Turn the light on your home network.

A calm guard for your home

Porchlight sits quietly in your tray and tells you, in plain words, when something changes at home: someone new joins your Wi-Fi, something pretends to be your router, or a setting leaves a door open.

  • It only looks. No logins, no password guessing, nothing that could disturb your devices.
  • It stays on this computer. No account, no cloud. Delete everything with one button.
  • It tells you what to do, one step at a time, and checks that the fix worked.
