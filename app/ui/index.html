Welcome to Porchlight

A calm guard for your home network

Porchlight lives quietly in your tray and tells you, in plain words, when something changes: a new device joins, something pretends to be your router, or your Wi-Fi has a weakness.

  • It only looks. No logins, no password guessing, nothing that could disturb your devices.
  • It stays on this computer. No account, no cloud. One file you can delete anytime.
  • It explains everything, how likely it is to matter, and exactly how to fix it.
