# Testing Porchlight

Thank you for trying Porchlight on your network. Twenty minutes of your time tells us more than any test on one
house can. Only test a network you're allowed to (your home, or one you look after).

## Install

1. Download the installer from https://porchlight-beryl.vercel.app/#app.
2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't signed by Microsoft yet: choose
   **More info → Run anyway**. You can compare the file's SHA-256 with the one on the download page.
3. Pick your Wi-Fi, tick "This is my network", and start. The first look takes about a minute.

## What to look at (about 20 minutes)

1. **Devices.** Does every device you own show up? Is each one named and recognised correctly (phone, TV,
   camera...)? Devices that don't announce a name say so: give them one.
2. **Findings.** For each item under "Needs your attention": is it true? Is the explanation clear to you? Did the
   fix work? Run a full checkup afterwards and see whether it disappears.
3. **New device alert.** Connect something that has never been on your network (a friend's phone, a laptop). You
   should get a notification within one check (10 minutes by default; "Check now" is faster).
4. **Wi-Fi shield.** Are your networks and your extender listed correctly? Any copies of your Wi-Fi that aren't?
5. **Leave it running** for a day or two. Did anything feel noisy, slow, or wrong?

## Telling us

- Something broke, or a result was wrong: open an issue at
  https://github.com/aboutbeyrem/porchlight/issues/new/choose and attach a diagnostic file
  (**Settings → Help → Save diagnostic file**). The file has counts and check names, never device names, network
  names or addresses.
- Prefer not to use GitHub? Send the diagnostic file and a few lines to the maintainer directly.

The questions that help most: *What confused you? What did you expect it to tell you that it didn't?*

## Removing it

Settings → Forget everything, then uninstall from Windows Settings → Apps.
